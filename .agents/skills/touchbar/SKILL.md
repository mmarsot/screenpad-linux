# Touch bar daemon

Owning files: `screenpad_touchbar/`, one module per concern, and `touchbar.py`, the launcher that runs its `cli.main()`. Installed as `/usr/local/lib/screenpad/`, with `/usr/local/bin/screenpad-touchbar` a link to `touchbar.py` there (see the [install](../install/SKILL.md) skill). The module map is in [docs/development.md](../../../docs/development.md#layout): `common` (constants, errors, runtime files), `codes`, `keys`, `items`, `config`, `defaults`, `config_edit`, `moves`, `migrate`, `scene`, `splitter`, `bar`, `pager`, `protocol`, `widgets`, `notify`, `asks`, `surface`, `apps`, `devices`, `fnkey`, `daemon`, `cli`. New code goes in the module of its concern; a module nearing 500 lines is split, not grown.

The client side of the app socket is `screenpad_app/` (`common`, `client`): the library apps use. It is installed next to the programs and is all the repository builds as a package (`pyproject.toml`), so an app in another repository installs it with pip from the git URL.

Read first:

- [docs/touchbar.md](../../../docs/touchbar.md): how the split, the bar, attention cards, notifications and the two pages behave.
- [docs/configuration.md](../../../docs/configuration.md): every `touchbar.toml` setting.
- [docs/app-api.md](../../../docs/app-api.md): the socket protocol for apps and questions.
- The renderer side: the [gnome-extension](../gnome-extension/SKILL.md) skill.

## Rules

- **Standard library only, run isolated.** The shebang is `#!/usr/bin/python3 -I`: isolated mode puts neither the script's directory nor the user's site-packages on `sys.path`, so `touchbar.py` adds the directory it really lives in (through the link) and imports only `screenpad_touchbar` from there. The package's modules use relative imports, and the imports go one way: `common` and `codes` import nothing of the package, `cli` is on top; an import cycle is a bug. A new module is picked up by `install.sh` (it copies the whole package).
- **A contact keeps the kind it started with** (`Splitter`): `Daemon._zone()` decides it once, when the contact starts, from the bar's height and the page: `BAR` above the bar, else `PAD` on the touchpad page and `SURFACE` on the apps page. A touch that starts in the bar never reaches the virtual touchpad, a touchpad touch that slides into the bar stays a touchpad touch, and a height change (the grip, an attention card) or a page turn applies only to new contacts. Only `PAD` contacts reach the virtual touchpad. It gets whole frames ending in `SYN_REPORT`; resync after `SYN_DROPPED`.
- **The pages** (`pager.py`): the swipe along the bar (`SWIPE_MIN`, `SWIPE_SLOPE`, `SWIPE_TIME` in `common.py`, one finger, on the bar only) and a tap on the page button turn them (the button shows only the page it goes to, `Pager.target()`, as an icon from `PAGE_ICONS`); `Pager.watch()` turns a swipe's lift into a `cancel` before the bar sees it. The apps page shows only while `Pager.available()` (grabbed, and the renderer at `PAGES_PROTOCOL`; the pages and the black are always on, `[screensaver] clock` and `[pager] return_after` are the only settings), and goes back to the touchpad after `return_after` seconds without a contact down, or at once when it can no longer show. Swipes with three or more fingers stay GNOME's, so the pager never claims them. The screensaver is drawn black by the extension; nothing here writes brightness.
- **The apps page** (`surface.py`, pages validated in `widgets.py`): the home page lists apps' pages, an app's page is a grid below the header under the bar's own height (`top`), and a card covers its top. The daemon does the hit-testing and sends `down`, then `tap`, `long` or `cancel` (`Apps.press`, to the page's owner even after it withdrew the page). Two safety rules: a touch that starts within `ASK_ARM` of a widget appearing, changing, moving, the page turning to or a card growing or going does nothing (`Surface.since`, keyed by `Spot.key()`); and a widget that changes or goes under a finger cancels that press. Widgets show and take touches only at `WIDGETS_PROTOCOL`; below it the page shows only its text.
- **Items are one model in two places** (`items.py`): a `[[bar]]` or `[[apps]]` block is exactly one of `key`, `command`, `app`, `tile` or `page`, and every kind goes in both (`Config.bar`, six spots; `Config.apps`, the home list's first eight cells). Code asks an item its `kind`, never where it is; `Bar` does what each kind does, on the bar and, through `Bar.act()`, from the apps page (`Surface` sends `down`, then `tap` or `cancel`). A tile goes in one place only (its events do not say where). The home list is the `[[apps]]` items, then the pages no `page` item places, growing past `HOME_ROWS` rows.
- **The click counts accepted presses only** (`clicks` in the scene, `Daemon._click`, the `click` callback of `Bar` and `Surface`): when the action happens, so a hold item on its touch (on the bar or the apps page), and on the lift that fires it for a tap item, an app's tile (`up` inside), an app, a page item, the page button, an answer or Dismiss, and a widget's `tap` or `long`. Never a slide away, a cancel, a swipe, the grip or the touchpad. The count lives on the daemon, so a new `Bar` keeps it; it only goes up.
- **The page button spot is last in `Config.spots`**, after the grip, so the other spots keep the indexes an extension from before tiles knows them by, and it is left out of the version 1 `spots`.
- **The daemon grabs only while `/run/screenpad/active` exists** (`screenpad on`) **and the display is on** (`Toggle.display_on()`: `bl_power` 4, or the `screenpad light` running), and only between touches; letting go releases every bar and pad contact first. Before letting go, and when the touchpad turns on with no grab (`Toggle.turned_on()`, Fn+F6's state 1 to 2), it resets the idle slots' tool types (`tool_reset()` in `devices.py`, [docs/touchbar.md](../../../docs/touchbar.md#how-it-works)): otherwise libinput keeps a palm it saw in a slot and one finger stops moving the pointer.
- **Fn+F6 never counts presses** (`fnkey.py`, [docs/touchbar.md](../../../docs/touchbar.md#fnf6-touchpad-and-display)): a press goes from the real state, GNOME's `send-events` and `bl_power`, by `next_state`. GNOME flips the setting on each press itself; the daemon waits for that flip (or `FLIP_WAIT`) and writes the setting only when it differs from the target (state 2 to 3). Only presses change the display: a touchpad turned off elsewhere leaves it as it is, and the next press goes from (touchpad off, display on) to state 2. The hotkeys device is read, never grabbed. The scene's `fnkey` (`Toggle.shown()`: `lit`, the presses that lit the display, and `lights_next`) drives the extension's ScreenPad on indicator; the loop republishes it only while not grabbed (held means lit), so it costs nothing while the bar is up. `Daemon.main()` makes the real `Toggle`; a `Daemon` made in a test has none unless it passes one, so tests never run `gsettings` or read the real backlight.
- **The config is strict**: unknown keys, settings and bad values are `ConfigError`, and the daemon then keeps the current bar. A new setting needs parsing, its errors, a line in `EXAMPLE_CONFIG`'s comments and in [docs/configuration.md](../../../docs/configuration.md), and tests.
- **The bar edits the config only in place**: `edit_config` rewrites the `height` line, adds, moves and removes app items, and moves items of any kind between `[[bar]]` and `[[apps]]` (`with_items`: kept items' blocks untouched and in order, a new app's block written, a moved item's block carried as it is under the other header (`carried`, `retitled`), each before the next kept item's comments or after the last block; the comments above a block taken away stay), keeps every other line and comment, and refuses an edit that would change anything else, reorder a list, or put one item in two places. Drops from the dock go only on an app's spot or an empty one (`drop` in `spot_tiles`); `add_app` replaces only an app on a full place.
- **Dragging items between the bar and the apps page** (`moves.py`, [docs/touchbar.md](../../../docs/touchbar.md#moving-items-between-the-bar-and-the-apps-page)) stays in the gesture model: `Bar` and `Surface` keep each contact by where it started and hand `Mover` its source `(place, index, item)` on moves and the lift; only a lift over the other place's home list or bar spots moves (`Mover.target`, `Surface.drop_cell`), cancelling the item's own action as a slide away. A free place moves at once; an occupied one (or a full list: its last item) asks through an `Ask` in the shared `Asks` (`Asker`) and Replace swaps, re-checked against the file by spec (`Mover.change`). No move to a cell within `ASK_ARM` of changing, under a card, off the home list, or that would put an app or page twice in a list; a reload ends the drag (the source item must be the same object). Feedback is classes only (`moving`, `target`, in `TILE_CLASSES` and `WIDGET_CLASSES`, styled in `stylesheet.css`) and the header's text, published only when `Mover.over` says the mark changed: no timer, no repaint while the finger stays in its place.
- **Changing the default config (`EXAMPLE_CONFIG`) needs a migration** (`migrate.py`): add the sha256 of the default being replaced to `OLD_DEFAULTS`, save that default as `tests/touchbar-v<N>-default.toml`, and add a migration test in `test_touchbar_migrate.py`. A default block that changed goes in `OLD_BLOCKS` (old text, new text), so an edited config gets the new block and keeps the rest; `convert_items` then turns an earlier version's `[[button]]` and `[[shortcut]]` blocks into `[[bar]]` items, only when the result parses to the same bar. Users' configs are never otherwise rewritten.
- **The scene is drawn without knowing tile kinds**: `touchbar.json` (`SCENE_VERSION`) is a list of tiles with geometry, what to draw, CSS classes and `pressed`. A new kind of tile is daemon-only work, but every `style` and class it uses needs a rule in the extension's `stylesheet.css` (tested).
- **The renderer handshake must match the extension**: `RENDERER_NAME`, `RENDERER_PROTOCOL`, `SOCKET_NAME`, the D-Bus `Launch` interface (all in `common.py`), the notify fields, urgency names, action ids and the notifications app name are mirrored in the extension's `contract.js` and `cards.js`. Bump `RENDERER_PROTOCOL` on both sides when an older extension would misdraw the scene, and gate the new feature on that protocol (as `ASKS_PROTOCOL`, `PAGES_PROTOCOL` and `WIDGETS_PROTOCOL` do), so what an older extension can draw keeps working.
- **Questions show only while they can be seen**: an ask is shown only while the touchpad is grabbed and `renderer.json` says the bar is drawn; otherwise the asker gets `unavailable`. A touch that starts within `ASK_ARM` (0.3 s) of a card growing does nothing, and the extension's growth animation is shorter than that. A card's tiles and its hit testing share one geometry, `ask_option_box` in `scene.py` (the text, a narrow Dismiss, then the answers up to the page button, at least as wide as the card is tall for one or two), so a touch hits what is drawn.
- **The app protocol is versioned** (`APP_PROTOCOL`, `MIN_APP_PROTOCOL` in `common.py`; `protocol_field` in `protocol.py`): a `hello` without `protocol` is version 1, every version from `MIN_APP_PROTOCOL` to `APP_PROTOCOL` is welcome, any other is the typed `unsupported-protocol` error. Bump `APP_PROTOCOL` when an app could otherwise not tell the bar lacks what it relies on (a new op or field apps send, a changed meaning), add the row to the version table in [docs/app-api.md](../../../docs/app-api.md#the-protocol-version), and keep the older versions working; a new field in what the daemon sends needs no bump. `screenpad_app.PROTOCOL` and its limits follow the daemon's (`test_the_library_speaks_what_the_daemon_does`).
- **The client library stays a library**: standard library only, Python 3.11+, and it imports nothing of `screenpad_touchbar` (`test_the_library_imports_nothing_of_the_daemon`); its tests run it against the daemon's own `AppServer`, so a change to `hello` or `welcome` on either side shows there.
- **A protocol change updates [docs/app-api.md](../../../docs/app-api.md)** and the app tests: ops, fields, limits (`MAX_APPS`, `MAX_LINE`, `MAX_OPTIONS`, `MAX_PAGES`, the home page's `HOME_COLUMNS * HOME_ROWS` pages in all, `MAX_WIDGETS`, the grid, timeouts), events and answer reasons.
- Notifications only mirror; a card's button presses the notification's own button and nothing else activates a notification. The mirror rules are `mirror_refusal` in `notify.py`.

## Tests

- One file per area, sharing `tests/touchbar_support.py` (the checkout on `sys.path`, axes, events, `Split`, `ConfigHome`, `FakeClient`, `Conn`, the bar, notify and page fixtures, Fn+F6's `FakeSetting`, `FakePanel` and `hotkey`): `test_touchbar_config.py` (keys, parsing items), `test_touchbar_edit.py` (writing in place, moved blocks), `test_touchbar_moves.py` (dragging items between the bar and the apps page, the Replace card, the swap), `test_touchbar_migrate.py` (earlier configs), `test_touchbar_items.py` (items of every kind on the apps page and page items on the bar, through the daemon), `test_touchbar_bar.py` (the split, the bar), `test_touchbar_scene.py` (tiles, any item in any spot, questions and app tiles in the bar), `test_touchbar_apps.py` (apps, the socket, the daemon), `test_touchbar_cards.py` (attention cards), `test_touchbar_clicks.py` (which presses click, and the extension playing it), `test_touchbar_notify.py` (notifications), `test_touchbar_pager.py` (the pages: swipes, the page button, routing by page, the return, their settings), `test_touchbar_surface.py` (apps' pages and widgets: validation, the home list, taps, long presses, arming, cancels, routing), `test_touchbar_commands.py` (`shortcut`, `status`, device discovery, the tool type reset on a model of the kernel's slots, the monitor), `test_touchbar_fnkey.py` (Fn+F6's cycle against a GNOME that flips the setting, `lit` and `lights_next`, the hotkeys device, the gsettings and screenpad calls through stubs, the grab following the display, the tool types reset when libinput reads the touchpad again), `test_touchbar_extension.py` (the extension against the daemon's constants), `test_touchbar_extension_widgets.py` (`widgets.js` on the apps page the daemon publishes). The client library's, sharing `tests/app_support.py`: `test_app_protocol.py` (the daemon's handshake, the library against the daemon's own `AppServer`, its constants against the daemon's), `test_app_client.py` (the library against the daemon's `AppServer`: version mismatches, tiles, pages, questions, `Link`, the import rule); `app_support.py` holds `DaemonBar`, the daemon's `Apps` served in a thread; it waits in `select` without its lock, since holding the lock across the wait starves the test's thread on Python 3.11 (its locks are not fair) and hung CI for minutes. A new test goes in the file of its area.
- They run with no root, no hardware and no uinput: events are fed as tuples, `ConfigHome` points `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` and `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` at a temporary directory for the config, the state files and the socket.
- [docs/development.md](../../../docs/development.md#tests) lists what the touch bar tests cover; keep it in step.
